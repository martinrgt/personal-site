> You play as a social worker working at the SROUC, helping students with their budget issues. Listen to what they have to say and search through their files. But you haven't much time, so you'll probably have to take wild guesses.

*Au SROUC* is a game that was made during the Social Game Jam, organized by [Félix Belthoise](https://felixbelthoise.itch.io/) in September 2023 with the association [Le Cœur et la Main](https://lecoeuretlamain.fr/). Their goal was to put together game developers and social workers in order to bring social work to the forefront.

{{< youtube 9bl4XDzw4Oc >}}

We made this game with Quentin, who worked as a social worker at the French CROUS. He helped students with their budgets and dealt with a lot of different cases.

We transposed his experience into this serious game. For every student, the player may choose among six clues, but only has 3 time units to do the job. Afterwards, two bonus time units are possible, but if they're spent, they won't be available to use for the next person.

At the end of the meeting, the player must pick a solution to the student's budget issue. But throwing money at issues isn't always the right solution — also because the amount of money is limited. Sometimes, giving food stamps or teaching the student how to manage their budget is enough to help them out of their situation.
