## At First, a Child Climbing a Tree
*Cosimo* is inspired by one of Italo Calvino's novels, *The Baron in the Trees*, where an 18th century young Italian nobleman decides to spend the rest of his life in the trees after a family quarrel.

The author himself presents his novel as concept-based. In a preface, Calvino explains that everything goes from the idea of a child climbing a tree and decides to never come down again. The rest of the novel, Calvino says, just extrapolates what this premise implies.

## Scientific Game Jam 2020
Like many others, this novel is to me video game material. It sets a simple rule — you must never touch the ground — and the characters act accordingly.

That's why I decided, during the Scientific Game Jam of 2020, to use it as a base to make a game where I explain how Calvino is a playful author.

🏆 The game won the Poetic Science Award.
