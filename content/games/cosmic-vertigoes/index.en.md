## Playing with Italo Calvino
It is my first iteration of turning Italo Calvino's work into video games. I worked with two friends, Leslie Astier and Lucas Friche, around a game that focuses on Italo Calvino's *Cosmicomics*.

I think that *Cosmicomics* are the first works to come to mind when mentioning playfulness in Calvino's work. The author uses physic-based theories and turn them into sensitive stories. He manages to create subjectivity where there isn't, through the memories of the old Qfwfq.

Qfwfq once was an amphibian who couldn't decide if getting out of the water and evolve into a mammal. But he also was there just before the Big-bang happened, stuck in the same spot with every other being.

In *Cosmicomics*, Italo Calvino gives the cosmos feelings, memories, and nostalgia.

## "If on a Summer's Night a Game Designer" Jam
This game was made for a [game jam organized by Antonio Amato](https://itch.io/jam/calvino2020), where he invited game designers to give their own vision of Italo Calvino through a game.

## A Bitsy Game
With Leslie and Lucas, we decided to use Bitsy to make this game. Bitsy is a minimalistic game engine that forces the users to make games based on simple ideas.

Although, we didn't stop there. The third part of the game, "I saw you", is a complex dialog system that we managed to shoehorn into the engine. It is a dialog with someone who lives on another planet across the galaxy, and the answers always arrive too late, making every efficient communication impossible. The original design was made with Twine.
