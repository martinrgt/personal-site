> Giovannino and Serenella explore the Italian Riviera devastated by World War II. By their playful approach of their world, they rebuild what was destroyed.

## Italo Calvino's at It Again
That's the third game inspired by Italo Calvino's work I've made with other people. This time, it's inspired by the short stories' collection *The Crow Comes Last*. It contains stories about how the people lived during the war: Resistance fighters or messengers, children that explore the ruins left by the war. 

Everyone has a particular way to live this strange moment, but the children use their playful attitude to reconquer those lost places. That's what we aimed to adapt into our game.

## Climb Ruins, Connect Dots
The game design is in two phases:
- Climb up the ruins to find a higher spot and enjoy the view.
- Once you arrive at the high spot, connect the dots and watch the ruin return to its original state.

## Scientific Game Jam 2021
This game was submitted to the 2021's edition of the Scientific Game Jam, which was focused on History studies.

With this game, I wanted to show how storytelling and literature are a way to  rebuild a common past and turn to a common future. That's how Calvino saw literature in those short stories.
