> You are supposed to write your paper. The only problem is… You don't feel like it. Go on and find every possible way to procrastinate!

During Spring 2021, I had to write a paper for a journal. I couldn't find the motivation to do it. Rather, there was this Ink software I'd been willing to learn in order to become a narrative designer. 

At the same time, some friends of mine hosted a paper jam to subvert the rules of academic publishing. Sounded like a match made in heaven!

![Screenshot](https://img.itch.zone/aW1hZ2UvMTA4MDEzNy82MjA1NzU5LmpwZw==/original/BqrONO.jpg "Screenshot of the beginning in 'Comment ne pas écrire son article'")

That's how this short interactive fiction was created. It ironically invites the player to procrastinate in every possible way in order to avoid opening a text document and doing something productive.

It is an Ink story I wrote to learn some advanced techniques.
