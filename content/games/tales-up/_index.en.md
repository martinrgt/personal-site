### Chose Your Own Adventure
*Tales Up* is a multiplayer roleplaying game where the players are invited into various adventures. Think of them as chose your own adventure books, but with smart devices and friends.

{{< youtube ApTra37WhPY >}}

### My Work on the Project
My work on *Tales Up* has covered every aspect of story creation, including:

- Delivering a **narrative strategy** where I presented the worldbuilding, the characterization, and the general stakes
- Crafting a **story flowchart** containing every conditional choice and branching path depending on each player's state
- Communicating with the artists and sound designers through a **moodboard** so that the music, sounds, and artworks perfectly match with the story intents
