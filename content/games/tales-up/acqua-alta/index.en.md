> As the sea level is rising, you venture away of the country side in order to reach one of the few remaining city in the mountain and find an aircraft to reach the airborne colonies. But you're not the only one with this idea.

In Venice, one says "acqua alta" when the laguna level is high enough to flood the city. I wanted to take this concept one step higher by submerging all the lands except the mountains. Hence the story of flying colonies and people fighting and stealing their way to them.

Of course, this story was motivated by the environment and climtate crisis we're living right now. But it was also a way for me to tell individual stories of people living their life and solving their problems even when their world is being flooded like never before.

The story came out in episodes, with cliffhangers and sides to choose. Will you be faithful to the army that wants to take over the airborne colony? Or will you defend it at the cost of your life?

- Of course, you can see among the influences the recently released *The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom*. But I also took inspiration from Ghibli's *Castle in the Sky*.
- I took the **character-based story** to another level with people looking for their disappeared parents, or seeking vengeance.
- Everything in the story is about **taking sides**. The players have to make decisive choice in order to ensure their own survival.
