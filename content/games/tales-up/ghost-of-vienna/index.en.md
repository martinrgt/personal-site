In "The Ghost of Vienna", I wanted to take the *Tales Up* formula into another place with modular structure. Since the mansion is, of course, haunted, the rooms can be visited in different order depending on the player's choices. The central lobby that leads to each room (the kitchen, the dining room, etc.) also changes depending on when the players visit it. 

The whole structure was designed in order to confuse players and make them lose track with reality, as if a ghost was behind all of it.

- My main references for this game were the **classic spooky ghost stories** such as *Casper* and *Luigi's Mansion*.
- I wanted to **open the structure of interactive stories** by letting the player which order they visit the house.
- This structure was perfect to integrate **puzzle situations** and break the rhythm.
