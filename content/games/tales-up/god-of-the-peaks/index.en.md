"God of the Peaks" is the first story I wrote for *Tales Up*. It is set in a place that is dear to my heart: the french-italian alps. That's where I spent all my summers, walking in the mountain and imagining magical creatures and surnatural places among the boulders and forest I would walk by.

- To write this story, I mixed together my love for mountain and well-known references as Ghibli's *Princess Mononoke*. 
- I also wanted to write a **non-violent story** where you don't have to hurt anyone to win. Instead, kindness and nature-awereness are essential.
- I wanted the experience to be as genuine as possible: expect to find some strong home-brew genepy.
