"Silver Dragons" is a road trip story. You and your friends are in a car and the smarter thing to do is to drive, because behind you a terrible storm is approaching. You'll visit locations and meet some scattered inhabitant in the Desert of Scales.

For this story, I wanted the branching paths to be quite literal: the players can't visit every place in one run, and there are a lot of hidden details and events that require you to make different choices every time.

This is the first *Tales Up* story with such rich characters. Every NPC has a past, a hidden goal that you can only discover by chatting with them while driving to the next destination.

I really loved the art style of this story. Actually, the lore changed based on the artistic choices made by the artist who decided to add ruins to the desert landscapes.

- **Road trip stories** are always full of wonder, mystery, escape from something. That's what I was aiming for.
- **Characters are central**: a lost researcher, an old lady that lived here all her life... If you listen to them carefully, maybe they'll help you.
- I added a **time-based mechanic** that pushes the player to make decisions and not explore everything in every location. Time's ticking, and the Dragons are approaching...
