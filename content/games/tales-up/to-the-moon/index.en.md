This story holds a special place into my heart. It is the story I wrote when I realized how fruitful my cultural and literary background could turn out to be in video games industry. *To the Moon* is inspired by the Italian epic poem by Ludovico Ariosto *Orlando Furioso*, that I've studied and loved. The story takes place during a war between Christians and the Saracen army, a classic frame in the epic poems from the 16th century.

The amount of events and situations and places the reader would discover in this story was impressive, and I had to make choices to figure what to keep and what to set aside. Of course, the part on the Moon could not be ignored.

- The story is inspired by an **Italian epic poem** from the 17th century and illustrated like a **children book**, with soft portraits and dream-like sceneries.
- The Moon is a place where are all the things that were lost on earth. It is a sort of reversed version of our world, a **mirror to our existence**.
- I wanted to explore different themes here, fragile illusions and dangerous dreams. **Appearances can be deceiving**.
