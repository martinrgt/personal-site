> It is your significant other one's birthday. But you can't think of any gift for them. Don't worry: get outside, take a stroll in the park, a museum, or maybe a bookshop. It'll remind you of shared memories and you'll come up with an idea.

{{< youtube "vDV_ovmhKxs" >}}

This game was submitted to the 2021 ink jam. The theme was "You'll think of something". With my team (Leslie Astier, Lucas Friche, Axel de Conti), we wanted to make something wholesome. A short walk in the shape of a game.

The art is willingly abstract so that the players can identify to the situation, no matter where they live.

As expected, this game was made with Unity and Ink. I had the opportunity to further experiment with ink, by making a narrative system that adapts to the order in which the player visits the different places. If you visit the bookshop at the end of the day, it won't be the same mood as if you visit it during the afternoon.

We put some particular attention to how the players feel when visiting a place. The sound design is here to give the players this sense of fulfilling loneliness when seeing in every day objects that significant other one you can't wait to see again.
